Subscriptions and purchases

Free trial. Receiva includes a 7-day free trial with every feature, up to 10 new invoices a day, once per Apple Account. Days the app isn't used still count. No payment details are needed for the trial.

Receiva PRO. After the free trial, or to add more than 10 invoices in a day during it, you can continue with Receiva PRO:

Monthly$4.99per month, auto-renewing
Yearly$39.99per year, auto-renewing
Lifetime$99.99one-time purchase, no renewal

Prices are shown in US dollars. The App Store shows the price in your local currency before you confirm.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your device Settings: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Your old invoices always stay viewable and exportable, even after a subscription ends.
  • To restore a purchase on a new device, use Settings → Receiva PRO → Restore purchases in the app.

Refunds. All payments are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Check your results

Receiva reads the store, date, total, currency and tax from your receipts automatically. Automatic reading can make mistakes. Please check the results and correct them before you rely on them.
